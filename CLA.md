## Why this exists (plain-language summary)

The whole editor is, and is meant to stay, **AGPL-3.0 — free and open source**. That never changes
for the code you contribute: it ships to everyone under the AGPL, exactly like the rest of the
Project.

The one extra thing this Agreement does is let the Maintainer keep **a full license to 100% of the
code**. That preserves two abilities the Project's strategy depends on:

1. **Offer a commercial exception** to organizations whose lawyers refuse the AGPL (dual-licensing) —
   a legitimate open-source funding model.
2. **Build the future paid/hosted layer** as a separate component on top of the AGPL core, without a
   single outside patch fragmenting ownership and foreclosing that path.

You keep your copyright. You are simply also granting the Maintainer a broad license to use your
Contribution — including in those ways. This is the standard "inbound=outbound **plus** a grant"
model (Apache-style), not an assignment: **you do not sign your work away.**

## How to sign

You accept this Agreement by either:

1. **DCO sign-off (preferred, lightweight).** Add a `Signed-off-by: Your Name <your@email>` line to

Either is sufficient. See [`CONTRIBUTING.md`](CONTRIBUTING.md) for the practical workflow.
